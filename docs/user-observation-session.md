# DeliveryBrief user observation session

Use one consenting person who prepares or reviews delivery reports. Explain that their comments will be recorded as notes and anonymized for the Quest. Video is optional and requires permission. Do not enter invented answers.

1. Ask the participant to prepare a client update manually from one anonymized week. Start a timer before collection and stop after their final review. Record errors and corrections.
2. Give them a comparable week in DeliveryBrief. Ask them to collect evidence, inspect it, generate, correct, approve, and download the PDF without guidance. Time the entire task, including corrections.
3. Ask what they checked, what confused them, what they changed, and whether they would use it next week. Record their actual words only with consent.
4. Repeat with three comparable pairs when possible. Counterbalance manual/app order to reduce practice effects.
5. Calculate reduction per pair as (manual minutes minus app minutes) divided by manual minutes. Report the median, sample size, and all raw times. The 60% target is pending until measured.

Record date, participant role, consent, source-week provenance, manual start/end, app start/end, interruptions, corrections, help given, and adoption decision. Keep personal identities outside the public repository.
